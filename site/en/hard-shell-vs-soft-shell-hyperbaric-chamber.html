Quick Comparison: Hard-Shell Commercial Systems vs Soft-Shell Bags

For commercial spas, longevity clinics, luxury hotels, and athletic performance clubs, choosing between a structural hard-shell chamber and a zippered textile soft bag directly affects operating pressure tiers, acoustic guest experience, throughput, and asset lifespan.

Choosing by Pressure Tier & Venue

Luxury hotels and recovery spas commonly start with Oslo or Dubai at Wellness or Professional tiers, whereas high-volume wellness clubs and clinical teams under physician supervision scale to multiplace Tokyo, Milano (3+1P), or Geneva (10+2P) setups with model-based safety engineering and target-market compliance documentation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Why do hotels and premium wellness clubs choose hard-shell chambers?

Rigid structural hulls support higher pressure engineering (up to 2.0–3.0 ATA Professional and 3.0–6.0 ATA Clinical models), CitySilent™ acoustic comfort, and architectural interior integration.

What oxygen concentration does the HBOT City Tech Series provide?

City Tech systems deliver 93–95% oxygen concentration alongside CityOS™ digital session management and 24/7 remote telemetry.

How do you verify compliance documentation for target markets?

Each chamber includes model-based safety engineering and compliance documentation tailored to the destination market's regulatory category.
