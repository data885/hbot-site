WHAT SETS US APART

Why Us Instead of the Far East?

We don't compete by cutting price — we compete by making our distributor profitable. Here is the difference, in concrete terms.

25–40 Days From the Far East. 4–5 From Us.

Shipments from Istanbul to the Gulf, the Turkic Republics and Europe are counted in days, not container schedules. Spare parts move just as fast. For a distributor that means selling without tying up capital in stock.

You Deal With the Manufacturer

No export agency or middleman in between. On a technical question you speak to the engineer who designed the cabin; on commercial terms, to the person who decides — and in the same time zone.

The Configurator Is Your Closing Tool

Sitting with your client, you build the cabin in their colour and interior and hand them a priced quote in the same meeting. Where competitors show a PDF catalogue and say "let me check the price," you close the sale.

Six Models, A Growing Range

From single-person cabins to a hospital-class chamber — home, clinic, hotel and hospital budgets from one catalogue. New models and system upgrades are in continuous development: your range grows without changing supplier.

You Are Not Alone on Certification

Certification requirements differ by country. We provide the technical file, engineering data and test documentation; registration and authorised-representative steps in your market are planned together.

Marketing Infrastructure Is Ready

A website in seven languages, model technical files, product films and a visual archive are made available to you. You won't lose months producing content from scratch.

HOW WE WORK

How We Stand Behind Our Distributor

We don't ship the product and step aside. Scope and commercial terms are settled in discussion, according to the size of the market.

Territorial Representation

For distributors who agree on performance targets, territorial priority and exclusivity are open to discussion.

Technical Training

Hands-on training for your team on installation, commissioning, user instruction and periodic maintenance.

Marketing Materials

Product imagery, films, technical files and digital campaign assets are made available in your language.

After-Sales Support

For spare parts, remote technical support and fault resolution you deal directly with the production team.

Showroom Terms

Special commercial terms for the demo cabin you display in your own showroom are open to discussion.

Export Operations

You work with our export team on shipping, packaging, customs documentation and delivery terms (Incoterms).

WHAT WE EXPECT

Who We Work With

We work with a limited number of companies per market. You don't need to meet every point below — but seriousness about representation is the quality we actually look for.

Sales experience in medical devices, wellness equipment or premium lifestyle products

The structure to handle import and customs processes in your market

A technical team for installation and maintenance, or the intent to build one

Existing customer relationships in clinics, spas, hotels, sports clubs or hospitals

A showroom or display area where the product can be presented

Willingness to work long-term with a focused, single-brand commitment

PROCESS

Evaluation Process

1. Application

Complete the form. Information about your company, your market and your experience is enough for the first assessment.

2. Meeting

With applications we find suitable, we hold an online meeting to clarify market potential and mutual expectations.

3. Pilot Order

We begin with a first order and a showroom cabin. At this stage pricing, delivery and support terms are put in writing.

4. Distribution Agreement

After the pilot stage, we agree on territory, targets and term, and sign the formal distribution agreement.
