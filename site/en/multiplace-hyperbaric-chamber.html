WHEN MULTIPLACE FITS

Plan capacity around the operating workflow

A multiplace hyperbaric chamber can be appropriate when a facility is planning shared sessions, professional supervision and a higher daily throughput. Capacity alone does not determine the configuration: available installation space, intended use, operator workflow, pressure requirement and destination country also belong in the initial brief.

Our project team starts with these practical questions before recommending a model. The final technical, delivery and documentation scope is confirmed for the individual project.

Questions to resolve before selecting a multiplace chamber

How many people should the chamber seat?

Base capacity on planned sessions, expected daily use, site circulation and supervision model—not on capacity alone.

What should be verified for the facility?

Confirm the installation area, access route, utilities, operating workflow and commissioning plan before finalizing a model.

How is safety and compliance handled?

Requirements are assessed for the specific model, configuration, intended use and target market. Review this with the project team before purchase.
