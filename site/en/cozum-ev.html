FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How much space do I need at home?

The chamber's outer dimensions and the access clearance around it are what matter. Dubai measures 120×110 cm and Oslo 240×110 cm outside. You can enter your room size in the planner above to see the approximate layout; the exact placement is confirmed during the site survey.

Will the chamber fit through my door?

The access route and door width are checked during the survey. Sharing dimensions, photos and the floor plan speeds up the assessment.

How loud is it during operation?

Oslo and Dubai run below 60 dB (CitySilent™).

What happens in a power outage?

All models come standard with a built-in backup power supply for power outages.

Which model suits a home?

Oslo stands out if you want reclining use; Dubai if you want a smaller footprint and seated use. Tokyo can be considered for two-person use.

Tell us about your space

Send us the room dimensions, intended use and expected number of users, and we will determine the right model and layout together.
