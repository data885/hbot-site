SAFETY & COMPLIANCE

Trust Built with Documentation, Not Slogans

Safety, intended use, training, maintenance and target-market compliance are assessed separately for every model.

Our safe-use approach

Installation and operation bring together manufacturer instructions, trained operators, monitoring, fire prevention, grounding, cleaning and scheduled maintenance.

  • Follow the instructions for use and defined intended use
  • Fire prevention and material control for oxygen-rich environments
  • Grounding, static-electricity and electrical-safety checks
  • Authorized staff training, monitoring and emergency procedures
  • Cleaning, consumables, service intervals and recorded safety checks

Project documentation package

Scope is confirmed by model, configuration, intended use and destination market.

  • Product identity and intended use
  • Model- and country-specific compliance package
  • Instructions for use and operator training
  • Maintenance, cleaning and service plan
  • Factory/acceptance tests, installation and commissioning
  • Fire-safety and emergency procedures

Regulatory disclosure

Regulatory status, classification and market availability vary by model, configuration, intended use and country. HBOT Chamber Tech makes compliance claims only for a verified model and market scope and does not make a blanket FDA-approval claim.

Official safety reference: U.S. FDA — Follow Instructions for Safe Use of HBOT Devices
