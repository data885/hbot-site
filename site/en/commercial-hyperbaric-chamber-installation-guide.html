Pre-Delivery Technical Checklist

  • Measure doorway, corridor turning radius, and elevator width along the delivery route.
  • Verify room ventilation / HVAC cooling to keep ambient room temperature comfortable during continuous CitySilent™ compressor cycles.
  • Plan 60 cm perimeter service clearance around equipment cabinets where site geometry allows (finalized during site survey).
  • Test your floorplan interactively using our Room-Fit Planner.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Which City Tech chamber fits a standard boutique room?

Dubai (110 × 120 × 180 cm) and Oslo (110 × 240 × 120 cm, 93 cm hull) are specifically sized to integrate cleanly into standard wellness suites.

Does Milano or Geneva require a site survey before dispatch?

Yes. Multiplace models such as Milano (225 × 440 × 240 cm) and Geneva (222 × 690 × 235 cm) require structural, access-path, and MEP verification before shipment.

Can I preview floor layouts online before contacting sales?

Yes. Open our interactive SVG Room-Fit Planner on the Wellness & Spa solution page to test placement inside real room dimensions.
