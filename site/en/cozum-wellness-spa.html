PROCESS

From discovery to the first session, one point of contact

  1. Discovery and planning

    Together we review the installation area dimensions, photos and access route, remotely or on site.

  2. Made to order

    You choose the model, exterior color and interior upholstery in the configurator, and we finalize the details together when you order.

  3. Installation and commissioning

    We install the chamber, run the tests and hand it over ready for use.

  4. Training and support

    Operating training is provided; remote monitoring with CityConnect™ and scheduled service come into play.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How many chambers does my spa need?

Share your daily session target, session length and opening hours, and we will work out the number of chambers and the layout together. The planner lets you see in advance how many chambers fit your space.

One multi-person chamber or several single-person chambers?

If group and couple sessions are your focus, Tokyo or Milano; if personalized programs are, several Dubai units are more flexible. Mixed setups are also possible.

Will the chamber match my spa's design?

Exterior color and interior upholstery are chosen in the configurator; special requests are settled together when you order.

Tell us about your space

Send us the room dimensions, intended use and expected number of users, and we will determine the right model and layout together.
