BEFORE YOU REQUEST A QUOTE

What to confirm with a clinic chamber supplier

Will the chamber fit the planned room and access route?

Confirm usable room dimensions, delivery route, utilities and site prerequisites as early as possible. These details are part of the project discussion.

Which model and market documentation applies?

Ask for documentation relevant to the precise model, configuration, intended use and target country. Requirements vary and must be confirmed for the project.

What happens after delivery?

Installation, commissioning, operator training and maintenance scope should be clearly documented in the project plan.

Discuss your clinic’s hyperbaric chamber requirements

Send the country, room conditions, expected capacity and project timing. Our team will help structure the next step.
