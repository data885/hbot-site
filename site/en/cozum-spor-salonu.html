PROCESS

From discovery to the first session, one point of contact

  1. Discovery and planning

    Together we review the installation area dimensions, photos and access route, remotely or on site.

  2. Made to order

    You choose the model, exterior color and interior upholstery in the configurator, and we finalize the details together when you order.

  3. Installation and commissioning

    We install the chamber, run the tests and hand it over ready for use.

  4. Training and support

    Operating training is provided; remote monitoring with CityConnect™ and scheduled service come into play.
