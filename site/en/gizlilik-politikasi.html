Privacy Policy

HBOT Chamber Tech, part of Almita Group, explains here what hbotchambertech.com collects, why, and who receives it. The text is written with Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Law No. 6698) in mind, and it also describes the rights visitors from other countries can exercise with us. First published on 30 August 2026.

What we collect

If you use the contact form, the distributor form, or the quote step in the configurator, we receive your name, email, phone number and message. The model, pressure and options you select travel with that request.

A visit also creates technical records: IP address, browser type, pages viewed, an approximate country, and clicks on the site. Those records are handled by the providers listed below.

Why we use it

We use this information to answer you, prepare a quote, continue a distribution conversation, understand how the site is used, and meet legal duties. We do not sell personal data.

Third-party services

The services that actually run on the site are listed below. No live-chat vendor is used.

Google Analytics 4 (measurement IDs G-C4BP4KX9FC and G-KZ5Y39XS2F) and Google Ads (AW-18410338370) measure visits and events such as forms, phone taps, catalog downloads and WhatsApp clicks.

Microsoft Clarity (project yamafw1sq8) records sessions, clicks and heatmaps. It is not loaded for browsers that look like bots.

Cloudflare delivers the site, applies security headers and runs the Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon. On Turkish pages only, a Cloudflare trace (cdn-cgi/trace) is read for the country code behind an optional language suggestion. That request is not used to build an advertising profile.

Formspree (formspree.io) delivers the contact and distributor forms. Some quote forms post to Google Apps Script. When the endpoint is available, a lead is also sent to crmalmita.com.

The configurator's currency hint reads the euro rate from open.er-api.com. Your name and message are not part of that call.

WhatsApp (WhatsApp (+90 501 950 7003)) opens Meta's WhatsApp with a short prefilled line. Your WhatsApp profile and that message are then handled under WhatsApp's own terms.

Selin, the on-site assistant, runs in your browser against prepared answers. The question you type is not sent to an external AI provider.

Cookies

Cookies support measurement and security. You can limit them in your browser. Some parts of the site may then work less smoothly.

Your rights and how long we keep data

You may ask whether we process your data, and you may ask us to correct or delete it, including under KVKK article 11. Write to info@hbotchambertech.com. We keep data for as long as the purpose, and any statutory retention period, requires.
